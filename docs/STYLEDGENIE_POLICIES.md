# StyledGenie Policies

## Shipping Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2025

### Where We Ship

- Germany: Standard and Express
- European Union outside Germany: Standard
- International outside the EU: Standard, with a minimum order value of EUR 70

Delivery windows are in business days, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays, and start after dispatch. Delivery times are estimates and can be affected by carrier or customs delays.

### Rates And Delivery Times

#### Germany

Standard shipping:

- EUR 0 to EUR 59.99: EUR 6.99
- EUR 60 and above: Free
- Estimated delivery: 0 to 14 business days

Express shipping:

- EUR 0 to EUR 79.99: EUR 14.99
- EUR 80 and above: Free
- Estimated delivery: 0 to 7 business days

#### European Union Outside Germany

Standard shipping:

- EUR 0 to EUR 70: EUR 12.99
- EUR 70 and above: Free
- Estimated delivery: 0 to 14 business days

#### International Outside The EU

- Minimum order value: EUR 70
- Shipping rate: EUR 12.99
- Estimated delivery: 0 to 21 business days
- Orders below EUR 70 are not eligible for international shipping

### Order Processing

- Typical processing time is 1 to 2 business days before dispatch
- Shipping confirmation with tracking is sent once the order leaves the facility

### Free Shipping Thresholds

Free-shipping thresholds of EUR 60, EUR 70, and EUR 80 are calculated after discounts and before shipping fees and taxes.

### Taxes, Duties, And Customs

- Germany and EU orders: VAT included where applicable and no customs duties for intra-EU deliveries
- International orders outside the EU: import duties, taxes, and local fees may apply and are payable by the recipient

### Address Issues And Undeliverable Parcels

Customers should enter their address carefully. Address changes after dispatch are not guaranteed. If a parcel is returned because the address was incorrect, undeliverable, or uncollected, StyledGenie can reship it, with extra postage if needed, or refund the items minus the original shipping charge.

### Split Shipments

Items may ship separately at no additional cost to speed up delivery. Separate tracking numbers will be provided.

### Carriers

Carrier selection is automatic and depends on destination, speed, and parcel size.

### Pre-Orders And Backorders

Orders containing pre-order or backorder items may ship when all items are available unless a split shipment is arranged.

### Chat Support And Self-Service

Customers can use the chat widget on styledgenie.com to:

- track an order by typing `Track order` and entering the order number and email
- open the shipping policy by typing `Shipping policy`
- browse FAQs by typing `FAQ` or asking questions such as `delivery time to Berlin?`

Chat is available 24/7. Human agents step in during business hours for complex queries.

## Refund And Return Policy

StyledGenie UG

At StyledGenie UG, customer satisfaction matters. If a purchase is not fully satisfactory, the refund and return policy provides a fair and transparent process.

### 1. Right Of Withdrawal

Consumers in the EU have the right to withdraw from a purchase within 30 days without giving a reason.

- The withdrawal period is 30 days from the day the order is received
- To exercise withdrawal, the customer must email info@styledgenie.com with the order number and details of the items to return

### 2. Return Conditions

To be eligible for return:

- the item must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with all tags attached
- lingerie, swimwear, earrings, and customized or personalized items are non-returnable for hygiene reasons
- returns must be shipped back within 14 days after notifying StyledGenie of the withdrawal

### 3. Return Process

1. Contact customer service at info@styledgenie.com within 30 days of receiving the order
2. Pack the items securely and include the original invoice or packing slip
3. Ship the parcel to the return address provided by the team

Customers pay return shipping unless the item is faulty or was delivered incorrectly. A trackable shipping service is recommended.

### 4. Refunds

- StyledGenie notifies the customer after receiving and inspecting the return
- Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days
- Shipping fees, customs duties, and handling charges are non-refundable

### 5. Exchanges

Direct exchanges are not offered. Customers should return the item for a refund and place a new order.

### 6. Damaged Or Defective Items

If an item is defective, damaged, or incorrect, customers should contact info@styledgenie.com immediately with photos. StyledGenie covers return shipping and issues either a full refund or a replacement.
